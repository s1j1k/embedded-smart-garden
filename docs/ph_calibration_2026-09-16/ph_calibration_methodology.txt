pH probe calibration methodology (plain text)
============================================
Session: 16 September 2026, ~19:19–19:27 AEST
Data: ph_calibration_timeseries.csv, ph_calibration_events.csv

Hardware and signal path
------------------------
A DFRobot SEN0169 industrial pH probe and meter board measure reservoir
acidity. The meter board outputs an analogue voltage in the 0–5 V range.
This signal is scaled into the ESP32 ADC range with a discrete 1 kΩ / 2 kΩ
resistive divider, so firmware reconstructs board voltage as:

  V_board = V_GPIO × 1.5

Converted pH uses the linear model:

  pH = m * V_board + b

where slope m and intercept b are determined by software two-point
calibration against standard buffers and stored in on-device flash
(LittleFS). Until a successful calibration is saved, the node uses the
DFRobot library factory defaults (m = -5.6344, b = 15.4516).

Calibration procedure
---------------------
Calibration was performed on 16 September 2026 using the Streamlit
dashboard wizard, which publishes MQTT commands
({"cmd":"ph_cal","action":...}) to the ESP32. The operator procedure was:

  1. Rinse the probe in distilled water and blot dry.
  2. Place the probe in pH 4.00 buffer and wait for V_board to settle.
  3. Capture the acid point via the dashboard (Capture acid).
  4. Rinse again, then place the probe in pH 9.18 buffer and wait.
  5. Capture the alkali point (Capture alkali).
  6. Save the fit (Save & exit), writing m and b to LittleFS.
  7. Return the probe to distilled rinse water as a qualitative check
     (distilled water is expected to read slightly acidic).

From the logged session:
  - Acid capture:  19:19:46 AEST, Va = 1.820 V → pH 4.00
  - Alkali capture: 19:23:34 AEST, Vb = 2.157 V → pH 9.18
  - Fit saved:     19:24:40 AEST

The firmware fitted the unique line through these two points (no imposed
slope polarity):

  m = (9.18 - 4.00) / (Vb - Va)
  b = 4.00 - m * Va

yielding m ≈ 15.370 and b ≈ -23.970. After save, with the probe in
distilled rinse water (~19:27 AEST), reported pH was in the mid-5 range,
consistent with slightly acidic rinse water.

Data logging used for documentation
-----------------------------------
Continuous sensor samples were retrieved from InfluxDB Cloud
(sensor_reading measurement, device garden-node-01) for the calibration
window. Calibration state markers (ph_cal_message, pending voltages,
fitted m and b) were aligned from concurrent MQTT telemetry archived by
the local dashboard. The combined export ph_calibration_timeseries.csv
and event summary ph_calibration_events.csv were used to generate
time-series figures of V_board and reported pH, with vertical markers at
acid capture, alkali capture, save, and the distilled-water check.

Notes for interpretation
------------------------
The two captured voltages in this session produced a positive slope
(Vb > Va while pH_b > pH_a). The software intentionally accepts the
empirical two-point line so that the documented operator captures are
exactly reproducible. Independent strip/reference checks of tank samples
remain useful to validate that buffer order and probe settle time were
correct before relying on the fitted line for closed-loop dosing.
